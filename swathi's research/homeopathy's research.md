🪄 Learn About Homeopathy

1. What is Homeopathy?
"Homeopathy is a holistic healing system based on the principle of 'like cures like.' It uses highly diluted substances to stimulate the body’s natural healing processes."
2. Key Principles 
Law of Similars : A substance that causes symptoms in a healthy person can cure similar symptoms in a sick person.
Minimal Doses : Remedies are highly diluted to minimize side effects and maximize effectiveness.
Holistic Approach : Treats the whole person—mind, body, and emotions—rather than just targeting symptoms.
3. Common Remedies
Arnica : For bruises, sprains, and muscle pain.
Arsenicum Album : For food poisoning, diarrhea, and anxiety.
Eupatorium Perfoliatum : For body pain during dengue or flu.
4. Sanskrit-Inspired Verse (Optional)
"Similia Similibus Curentur"
Translation :
"Let likes be cured by likes."
5. Significance : The foundational philosophy of homeopathy.
Modern Relevance
Homeopathy is widely used for chronic conditions like allergies, migraines, and skin disorders. It complements modern medicine by offering gentle, long-term solutions with minimal side effects.
