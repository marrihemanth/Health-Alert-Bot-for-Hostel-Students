MODERN MEDICINE

Website : https://www.cdc.gov
Why Use It? : A trusted resource for understanding diseases, their causes, and prevention strategies.
What You’ll Find :
Seasonal disease information (e.g., flu, dengue).
Evidence-based treatment guidelines.
3. Mayo Clinic
Website : https://www.mayoclinic.org
Why Use It? : Offers practical, patient-focused information on modern medicine.
What You’ll Find :
Symptom checkers and treatment options.
Insights into the latest medical technologies and therapies.
