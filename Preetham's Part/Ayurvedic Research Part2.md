1. What is Ayurveda?
"Ayurveda is the world’s oldest holistic healing system, originating in India over 5,000 years ago. It focuses on balancing the body, mind, and spirit through natural practices. Its principles are timeless, emphasizing harmony with nature."

2. The Three Doshas
Ayurveda revolves around three energy types (Vata, Pitta, Kapha ):

Vata : Governs movement (e.g., breathing, circulation).
Imbalance : Anxiety, dry skin, irregular digestion.

Pitta : Controls metabolism and heat.
Imbalance : Heartburn, anger, inflammation.

Kapha : Maintains structure and lubrication.
Imbalance : Weight gain, congestion, lethargy.

3. Key Principles
Balance is Health : Disease arises from imbalanced doshas.
Prevention Over Cure : Focus on diet, herbs, and lifestyle.
Personalized Care : No two individuals are alike; treatments are tailored to your dosha.

5. Sanskrit Verse (Optional)
Samadosha samagnisch samadhatu malakriya
Prasanna atma mana swastha iti abhidhiyate.
Translation :
"Health is balance—balanced doshas, digestion, tissues, and waste. A joyful soul, mind, and senses define true wellness."

6. Modern Relevance
Ayurveda is resurging globally, validated by scientific research. It complements modern medicine for holistic care.
